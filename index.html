    How to Play

    Got inspired by Boggle, Mark Brown's fantastic game, Word Play, threw together a mash-up with Scrabble's point system, and common English letter frequencies.

    Mots is designed to be a quick game you can pick up, play for 5 minutes, and put down—ideal for short daily pauses. Or for binging, but I tried to keep it a light game where you don't need to plan much ahead. Ideal for pauses that should happen daily. Eat more fiber.

    • The game has infinite rounds. But each round requires more points to pass. Where can you get?

    • There are black tiles in the letter bag. More each round, but eventually they stop. For now, I may change that.

    • You start with 10 plays. You gain 4 extra plays after each round and also with some powerups.

    • Words must be at least 4 letters long, in your chosen language. As long as you choose one of the supported ones, that is.

    • After each round, choose one of two power-ups. Choose wisely!

    • Sometimes the last positions of the answer section will not be usable. Life is hard, this game eventually is too.
